1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

- Platform licence (per seat) [PLAT-BASE]: 131 units at USD 39.27 per unit per month

Fees shall increase by 3.5% on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3. DISCOUNTS

Customer will receive 10% off the contracted price of SMS messages bundle for invoices dated on or before 31 December 2024.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 50,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.
