1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

2. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
Pricing is subject to a yearly adjustment of 7% commencing 01 July 2026.

3. DISCOUNTS
Customer will receive 15% off the contracted price of SMS messages bundle for invoices dated on or before 28 February 2026.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 100,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.
The monthly allowance for API calls bundle is 250,000 units; excess units are charged at 0.006 USD each.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.
