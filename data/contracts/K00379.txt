1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

- API calls bundle [API-CALLS]: USD 831.55 per month
- Data storage (per TB) [DATA-STOR]: 28 units at USD 52.11 per unit per month
- SMS messages bundle [SMS-MSG]: USD 385.00 per month

2. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
The Supplier may apply an annual uplift of 5% to the Fees, effective from the first anniversary (01 May 2026).

3. DISCOUNTS
A promotional discount of 15% applies to Data storage (per TB) until 31 August 2025.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
API calls bundle includes up to 500,000 units per month. Usage above this allowance is billed at USD 0.006 per unit.
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 50,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Customer's registered country.
