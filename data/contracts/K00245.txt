Registered country: Netherlands
Effective Date: 01 January 2024
Term: until 31 December 2026

1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

- Data storage (per TB) [DATA-STOR]: 158 units at USD 54.98 per unit per month
- SMS messages bundle [SMS-MSG]: USD 354.68 per month
- API calls bundle [API-CALLS]: USD 772.43 per month

2. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
The Supplier may apply an annual uplift of 3.5% to the Fees, effective from the first anniversary (01 January 2025).

3. DISCOUNTS
Customer will receive 10% off the contracted price of SMS messages bundle for invoices dated on or before 31 March 2024.
A promotional discount of 5% applies to API calls bundle until 31 August 2024.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 100,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.
API calls bundle includes up to 250,000 units per month. Usage above this allowance is billed at USD 0.006 per unit.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.

6. GENERAL
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Customer's registered country.
