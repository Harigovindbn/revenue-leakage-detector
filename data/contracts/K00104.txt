1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

Fees shall increase by 6% on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

Customer will receive 15% off the contracted price of Premium support (flat) for invoices dated on or before 28 February 2026.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
SMS messages bundle includes up to 20,000 units per month. Usage above this allowance is billed at USD 0.015 per unit.
API calls bundle includes up to 500,000 units per month. Usage above this allowance is billed at USD 0.006 per unit.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.
