1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

- Data storage (per TB) [DATA-STOR]: 157 units at USD 57.63 per unit per month

2. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
Fees shall increase by 3% on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 20,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.
The monthly allowance for API calls bundle is 500,000 units; excess units are charged at 0.006 USD each.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.
