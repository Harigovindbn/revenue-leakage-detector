Registered country: Ireland
Effective Date: 01 March 2025
Term: until 29 February 2028

1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

- Platform licence (per seat) [PLAT-BASE]: 176 units at USD 39.23 per unit per month
- SMS messages bundle [SMS-MSG]: USD 399.01 per month
- Data storage (per TB) [DATA-STOR]: 143 units at USD 52.84 per unit per month

2. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
The Supplier may apply an annual uplift of 5% to the Fees, effective from the first anniversary (01 March 2026).

3. DISCOUNTS
Introductory pricing: Platform licence (per seat) is discounted by 15 percent through 30 November 2025, after which standard rates apply.
A promotional discount of 5% applies to SMS messages bundle until 31 October 2025.
Introductory pricing: Data storage (per TB) is discounted by 20 percent through 31 May 2025, after which standard rates apply.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 100,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Customer's registered country.
