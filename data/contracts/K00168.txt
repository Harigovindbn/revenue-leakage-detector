1. SERVICES AND FEES
The Customer subscribes to the following services. Fees are in USD, exclusive of tax, billed monthly in arrears.

- Platform licence (per seat) [PLAT-BASE]: 173 units at USD 38.88 per unit per month
- API calls bundle [API-CALLS]: USD 823.25 per month
- SMS messages bundle [SMS-MSG]: USD 342.61 per month

2. PRICE ADJUSTMENT
Fees shall increase by 5% on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

3. DISCOUNTS
Customer will receive 5% off the contracted price of Platform licence (per seat) for invoices dated on or before 31 December 2024.

4. USAGE ALLOWANCES
API calls bundle includes up to 250,000 units per month. Usage above this allowance is billed at USD 0.006 per unit.
Overage: any SMS messages bundle usage beyond 100,000 units in a calendar month incurs a fee of USD 0.015 per additional unit.

5. TAXES
All Fees are exclusive of GST, which will be charged at the applicable rate.

6. GENERAL
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Customer's registered country.
